# AI-human collaboration

The division of responsibility is deliberate:

- The human defines hypotheses, metrics, risk thresholds, and judgments about
  economic plausibility.
- AI generates candidate transformations, automates experiments, summarizes
  results, and detects duplicates.
- The system tracks provenance, evaluates candidates, applies gates, and ranks
  the surviving set.

AI is a research multiplier, not a black-box strategy generator. The synthetic
demo is designed to make that boundary visible: a person can inspect the
candidate specification, checks, backtest convention, and every output file.
